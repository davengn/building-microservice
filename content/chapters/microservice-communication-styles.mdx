## Why this lesson matters

Services are only independent if their conversations are well chosen. This lesson helps you connect communication style to coupling, failure behavior, and team ownership.

- A call graph is also a dependency graph.
- Events can decouple timing while still coupling meaning.
- Contracts need ownership and versioning habits.

## Core study path

Study each communication style by asking what happens when the receiver is slow, unavailable, or evolving independently.

- Use request-response for direct questions that need immediate answers.
- Use events when facts can be published and consumed independently.
- Use workflow coordination when business progress must be explicit.

## Practice cues

For any service interaction, write down who needs the answer, how quickly they need it, and what should happen if the other service is down.

- Identify the consistency requirement before choosing a protocol.
- Look for chains of synchronous calls that amplify latency.
- Check whether event names describe business facts or technical noise.
