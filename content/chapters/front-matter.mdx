## Why this lesson matters

This opening note explains how to use the study site: read the public lessons for orientation, follow source references for private review, and keep the emphasis on your own understanding.

- The site is arranged as a study path, not a reproduced book.
- Each lesson favors summaries, decisions, and review prompts.
- Source-backed context stays traceable without becoming a public asset.

## Core study path

Start with the first chapter when you want the conceptual foundation. Jump to later chapters when you need a specific operating concern, such as testing, observability, resiliency, or organization design.

- Read the chapter summary before the sections.
- Use the takeaways to check whether the lesson is worth deeper study.
- Mark progress locally when the lesson is no longer new.

## Practice cues

Before moving on, decide what kind of reader you are today: exploring the idea, preparing to apply it at work, or reviewing for recall. That frame changes what you should pay attention to.

- If exploring, scan the sequence and vocabulary.
- If applying, look for tradeoffs and failure modes.
- If reviewing, use the takeaways as prompts.
