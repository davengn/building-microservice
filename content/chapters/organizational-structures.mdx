## Why this lesson matters

Microservices are as much about coordination as code. This lesson examines whether team boundaries, responsibilities, and platform support match the architecture you want.

- Service ownership should include build, run, and evolution responsibilities.
- Team dependencies can become architectural dependencies.
- Shared standards should help teams move, not freeze them.

## Core study path

Read by mapping services to teams. For each service, ask who decides changes, who handles incidents, and who pays for operational complexity.

- Prefer clear ownership over shared ambiguity.
- Use platform capabilities to remove repeated work.
- Keep governance lightweight and evidence-based.

## Practice cues

Draw your team-service map and mark the interactions required for one feature change.

- Count approvals and handoffs.
- Name the team that owns each production alarm.
- Decide which shared capability belongs in a platform.
