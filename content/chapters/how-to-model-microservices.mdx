## Why this lesson matters

Microservice design starts before code. This lesson focuses on finding boundaries that reflect the business vocabulary, rate of change, and ownership model.

- Domain terms reveal where people already expect different rules.
- Shared databases can hide coupling that service diagrams miss.
- Modeling work should expose tradeoffs before teams commit to extraction.

## Core study path

Study the chapter as a boundary workshop. Move from nouns and workflows toward ownership, invariants, and integration contracts.

- Identify capabilities before assigning technology.
- Separate local consistency needs from eventual integration needs.
- Ask which decisions should be owned inside one team.

## Practice cues

Pick a familiar product area and draw two possible boundaries. For each boundary, describe what becomes easier, what becomes slower, and what failure mode becomes more visible.

- Look for data that changes for different reasons.
- Watch for workflows that cross too many ownership lines.
- Prefer boundaries that can be explained without implementation details.
