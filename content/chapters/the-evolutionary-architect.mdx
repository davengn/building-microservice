## Why this lesson matters

Service systems change continuously. This lesson focuses on architecture as a feedback practice that helps teams make good decisions without constant central approval.

- Principles should guide real choices, not decorate slides.
- Fitness functions turn architectural concerns into signals.
- Guardrails should be reviewed as the system learns.

## Core study path

Read with the question: how do you keep useful consistency while preserving team autonomy?

- Define the qualities that matter for the system.
- Create tests, dashboards, or reviews that make those qualities visible.
- Update constraints when evidence changes.

## Practice cues

Pick one architecture principle and turn it into an observable check. Decide who owns the check and what happens when it fails.

- Avoid rules that no one can measure.
- Use exceptions as learning moments.
- Keep governance close to delivery feedback.
